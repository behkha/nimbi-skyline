A menu bar weather app in the nimbi family. Four things to decide before any view code: the menu bar glyph, the dropdown, the island page and the app icon. Each row shows the options drawn with the real tokens, cloud and limits from DESIGN.md. The sample is Oslo at 14:20 with light rain arriving around 15:00.

Data and limits

MET Norway Locationforecast 2.0 (compact), through a small Cloudflare Worker that caches by ~1 km cells, honours Expires and keeps your location away from MET. Hourly steps, so “soon” means “within the hour”. Minute-level nowcast exists only in the Nordics; it can come later.

Attribution “Weather data © MET Norway” sits in the dropdown footer and the About window.

Open question for DESIGN.md

Rain amounts need a color. The options here use sky #7FD0FF as a flat color (never the aurora gradient). If you agree, it gets added to 4.5 as a rain state token. Also 18.5 says weather is never needsYou; the storm row above is the one proposed exception.

The island page

For phase 2 of 18.3: Skyline publishes data, the island host draws it. The closed island and the page header are the same in every option; the options differ in the detail block (18.4: bars, rows or ring). Until then Relay owns the notch and Skyline stays in the menu bar.

Decision 4 of 4

App icon

The family tile from 11.2: the same aurora cloud on the dark squircle, eyes looking right, exactly one prop. Shown at 256 and at 32 px, where props usually fail.

A · Raindrops

Recommended
  • Says the app's one job, “rain is coming”, and matches the island prop.
  • Three sky-blue drops survive at 32 px.
  • A crying-cloud reading is possible; the drops sit low-left, away from the eyes, to avoid it.

B · Sun behind

  • Warm, unmistakably weather.
  • The classic “partly cloudy” icon every weather app has.
  • Amber reads as “waiting on you” in nimbi's state colors.

C · A skyline below

  • The name, drawn; nobody else's icon looks like this.
  • Says “city” before it says “weather”; the rooftops turn to mush at 32 px.
