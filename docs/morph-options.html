Skyline · the shape-shifting cloud · options · 11 Oct 2026

The cloud becomes the weather

Sun when it's sunny, a raindrop in the rain, a bolt in a thunderstorm, a snowflake, a crescent moon at night. The face, the eyes and the aurora skin stay, so it's always the same little cloud, only in a different shape. Pick a weather and watch the three approaches below.

DESIGN.md 3.5 and 16 say the cloud's outline stays unchanged in every app. Any of these needs a new line there: "Skyline's cloud may take the weather's shape; skin, face, eyes, moods and gaze stay."

A · Shape-shifter

Recommended
  • The weather at a glance, from across the room: you see a raindrop, not a cloud with drops.
  • Every change of weather is a little show (a spring morph, about 0.6 s).
  • The furthest from the family's cloud; Skyline looks least like Relay.

B · Same cloud, weather around it

  • Within DESIGN.md as it is: marks and props next to the cloud, which is how the moods already work.
  • Mild: a sun crown and falling drops say less than becoming the sun.

C · Morph, then come home

  • The shape-shift happens when the weather changes or you open the dropdown, then it settles back into the cloud (with B's props).
  • The family cloud most of the time; the trick stays a surprise.
  • You can miss it: open the dropdown a second later and it's already a cloud again.
