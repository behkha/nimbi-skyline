Skyline · redesign options · 11 Oct 2026

Skyline, inspired by the two-pane card

Three ways to bring the reference's feel into Skyline's dropdown: the big temperature and condition, sun times and facts with line icons, days as cards, colored weather symbols, and a big soft cloud in the corner. All mockups use Oslo on a Saturday afternoon.

Take
  • Big temperature with high and low under it.
  • Condition in big uppercase ("PARTLY CLOUDY").
  • Sunrise, sunset, wind, humidity as line-icon facts.
  • Days as cards, today in a raised tile.
  • Place in caps with the date and local time.
Adapt
  • The corner cloud becomes nimbi's own cloud, peeking in, still in its mood and following the cursor.
  • 3D emoji icons become SF Symbols in multicolor (11.1 allows only SF Symbols).
  • The eclipse orb becomes the cloud itself; nimbi never puts a second character on screen.
Leave out, or ask
  • The blue gradient on 24°C: 4.4 keeps gradients off product text. Flat white, unless you want an exception (decision 4).
  • Skyline's own pieces stay: the 24 h skyline chart, the conditions strip and grid, keys, MET's credit.
