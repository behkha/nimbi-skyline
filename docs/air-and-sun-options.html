Switch to MET's complete product

  • Feels like: MET's own apparent temperature.
  • Wind and gusts: gusts weren't in compact.
  • Rain chance: per-hour probability, plus thunder probability.
  • UV index: MET gives it for a clear sky, so the label says "UV up to 6".

New source

Air quality and pollen from CAMS

  • Air quality: worldwide, hourly. European AQI (Good … Extremely poor), or US AQI in the US.
  • Pollen: Europe only (alder, birch, grass, mugwort, olive, ragweed). Elsewhere the row stays away.
  • Via Open-Meteo's free API: no key, CC BY 4.0, non-commercial use only under 10k calls a day. A paid Skyline would need their subscription or CAMS directly.
Credit line: "Air quality: CAMS via Open-Meteo".

What to fetch, and what may nudge you

2 · Weather extras (MET)
Feels like, wind and gusts, rain chance, UV: all four.
Rain chance also tints the chart: a faint column behind hours with 30% or more.
3 · Air and pollen
CAMS via Open-Meteo, cached an hour, with its credit line.
Or skip air and pollen and stay MET-only.
4 · Nudges
Air "Very poor" or worse, and UV 8+, are notice: the island comes forward, amber chip. Never needsYou, never the cloud's mood.
Or: shown only, never nudges.
